=== Bug Fixes ===

- Industrial crops are no longer experimental
    - Place crop sticks on farmland and plant seeds on them, or place a second crop stick to crossbreed neighboring crops into new crops
    - Crops can mutate, react to items used on them and can have requirements like soil, light level or a block below the soil
    - Weeds grow on empty crop sticks, so plant something on them quickly
    - New crops: Stick Reed, Golden Dandelion, Iron Vines, Tin Blossom, Coprium (oxidizes over time, can be waxed with honeycomb), Wither Rose, Dandelion, Poppy, Ender Lily, Red Mushroom and Brown Mushroom
    - Wheat, Carrots, Potatoes, Beetroots, Melons, Pumpkins and Nether Wart can be grown on crop sticks
    - Crop crossbreeding recipes are shown in JEI
    - Farmland with crops on it can't be trampled
- New tool: Crop Analyzer, hold right-click on a crop to see its stage, drops and requirements
- New machine: Crop Manager, harvests crops, removes weeds and applies bone meal in an area around it
- New machines: Low, Medium, High and Extreme Voltage Transformers
- Added machine upgrades: Overclocker, Energy and Transformer Upgrade, which can be managed in the new upgrade panel of machine guis
- The Mining Laser is now functional: it mines blocks and damages entities with a laser beam (range, speed and damage are configurable)
- The Electric Hoe can harvest crops by right-clicking them and now uses energy when breaking blocks
- Added Tin and Copper Nuggets
- Machines now play a sound while running
- Machines now react to redstone changes right away
- Burnt machines can't be opened anymore and tell you to repair them by right-clicking them with a Fuse
- Cables now also connect to blocks that only use FE
- Fixed energy not being transported correctly through blocks that only accept energy on some sides, like transformers
- Renamed Stick Resin to Sticky Resin
- Added config options for the Crop Manager, Crop Analyzer, Mining Laser and the machine upgrades
- Added a Guide book when GuideME is installed (GuideME is optional)
    - Craftable from a book, a refined iron ingot and redstone, and can be found in dungeon chests
    - Contains a list of all industrial crops
- Added Toolbox
    - Stores up to 8 tools, right-click tools onto it to store them
    - Scroll while hovering it to select a tool, right-click with an empty cursor to take it out
    - Press C while holding it to set filters for its slots, also supports dragging items from JEI
    - Right-click it to collect all tools from your inventory that fit into its filtered slots
- Added Sugar Cane crop
- Added Sweet Berry Bush crop
- Reworked Stick Reed
    - New Stick Reed item and textures, the crop now has 9 growth stages
    - Drops 2-3 Stick Reeds, which are also its seed
    - Crossbreed Sugar Cane and Wheat to get Stick Reed
    - Extract into 2 Rubber with a 75% chance for a third one, macerate into 3 Sugar, compress 3 into 7 Paper or craft 2 into a Plant Ball
- Sugar Cane is now turned into Sugar in the Macerator instead of the Extractor
- Crop mutations and crop interactions are now shown in JEI
- Dungeon and village chests now contain Industrial Recrafted loot
- The Crop Analyzer now counts growth stages from 1 (e.g. 9/9 instead of 8/8)
- The Crop Analyzer now shows the correct soil and light requirements for vanilla crops and hides the light requirement for crops that grow at any light level
- The Basic and Advanced Energy Storage Units now show their own name in their gui instead of "Battery Box"
- The Wrench is now part of the c:tools/wrench tag
- Removed the Cutter
